Cargo Rush ("the app") is a fleet management app for haulage operators,
published by {COMPANY}. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and
what happens to it.

The app is used by two kinds of people: **staff of a haulage firm** (drivers
and office staff, whose accounts are created for them by their employer) and
**customers** booking a delivery. What is collected differs slightly, and is
noted where it does.

## What we collect

**Account details.** Your name, email address and password. Customers who
register themselves may also give a contact phone number. Your password is
never stored in readable form.

**Device name.** A label such as "cargoApp android" is recorded against your
sign-in so you can tell your devices apart, and so signing in on a new handset
does not sign you out of another.

**Location, including in the background — drivers only.** While a driver has
an active trip, the app reports the vehicle's position so the dispatch office
can see where a load is and give the customer an accurate arrival time.

- Collection **starts** when a driver taps to start a trip.
- Collection **stops** when that trip is delivered or cancelled.
- It never runs when no trip is active, and never for customer accounts.
- A permanent notification is shown for as long as it is running.

Background collection is necessary because a delivery runs for hours with the
phone cradled and the screen off. Location gathered only while the app is
open would report nothing for most of the journey.

**Photographs.** Drivers photograph a delivered load as proof of delivery, and
may attach photos to a vehicle inspection or an incident report. The app
requests camera and photo library access only when you use those features. It
takes still images only — it does not record video or audio.

**Operational records you enter.** Trip progress, delivery logs, vehicle
inspections and incident reports.

## How it is used

Solely to operate the service: signing you in, dispatching and tracking trips,
proving deliveries, recording inspections and incidents, and producing the
related records for the haulage firm you work for or are a customer of.

We do **not** use any of it for advertising or profiling, and we do **not**
sell it.

## Who it is shared with

Your data is held by {COMPANY} and made available to the haulage firm whose
account you belong to. A driver's trip location and proof-of-delivery photos
are visible to that firm's dispatch and office staff, and delivery status is
visible to the customer for their own load.

It is not shared with anyone else, other than service providers who host the
system on our behalf, or where the law requires it.

## Security and retention

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and held on access-
controlled servers. Records are kept for as long as the haulage firm's account
is active, or as long as tax and transport law requires — whichever is longer.

## Your choices

- **Location** can be revoked at any time in Android Settings → Apps → Cargo
  Rush → Permissions. Trip tracking will then stop working; the rest of the
  app continues to function.
- **Camera and photos** can be revoked the same way. You will not be able to
  submit proof of delivery.
- **Access, correction or deletion** of your data: email {CONTACT_EMAIL}, or
  ask your firm's office. We respond within 30 days. Some records must be
  retained where law requires it, and we will tell you if that applies.

Deleting the app removes it from your phone but does not delete records held
by your firm — use the route above.

## Children

Cargo Rush is a workplace tool and is not directed at, or intended for, anyone
under 18.
